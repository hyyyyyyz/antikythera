# Gallery artwork

Jason Huang’s personal calendar uses the red Gaia’s Vengeance Vandal from VALORANT. The tree roots, hollow stock and red crystal in the illustration come from **Gaia Vandal (Red)**, a 3D model published by **ILilMitch**. The model page credits the underlying assets to Riot Games and lists the contributor’s work under CC BY 4.0 for the stated personal and educational use. [Model and creator](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/gaia-vandal-red-dfeddd540e7641bfb0b7128155117a1d) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) · [Original skin artist’s portfolio](https://polster.artstation.com/projects/qQYZoD)

Antikythera took a transparent three-quarter view through Sketchfab’s public Viewer API. We cropped the render to the silhouette, traced its colors into SVG paths and placed it between two layers of the Panda gripper. The resulting plate appears in the gallery animation and Jason’s profile. The game artwork belongs to Riot Games and is outside the repository’s MIT license. [Rendered plate](gaia.svg) · [Viewer API](https://sketchfab.com/developers/viewer/functions)
